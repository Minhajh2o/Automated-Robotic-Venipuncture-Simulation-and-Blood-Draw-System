# **THE TRUST FABRIC: A CUSTOMER-CENTRIC PRIVACYENHANCING AI ARCHITECTURE** 

_Executive Concept Paper | Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) Alignment | New Telecom Ltd._ 

## **1. Deconstruction of the Problem** 

New Telecom Ltd. is transitioning from a legacy connectivity provider into an AI-driven digital services ecosystem (GenAI customer care, automated fraud detection, churn prediction, and hyper-personalized marketing). However, privacy activities remain fragmented across business silos, engineering squads, and third-party vendors. While cybersecurity controls are mature, senior management faces critical blind spots: personal data resides in disparate data lakes, AI systems consume sensitive metadata without dynamic tracking, and customer payloads risk unauthorized cross-border egress. The enactment of the Bangladesh Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) converts these operational gaps into severe statutory liabilities. 

## **2. Key Privacy, Security & Ethical Challenges** 

- **Children's Privacy (PDPA Mandate):** Shared family and student SIMs mask underage usage; unmitigated AI engines risk illegally tracking, profiling, and directing targeted offers at minors (<18). 

- **Shadow AI & Data Sovereignty:** Product squads leverage offshore cloud AI APIs, risking unauthorized egress of subscriber PII and breaching statutory data localization requirements. 

- **Black-Box AI & DSAR Friction:** Complex fraud and churn models lack transparency. Crucially, statutory Right to Erasure requests cannot purge personal data embedded in trained weights without full retraining. 

- **Adversarial AI Attack Surface:** Customer-facing GenAI assistants and e-KYC pipelines are vulnerable to prompt injections, training poisoning, and model inversion attacks that exfiltrate subscriber identities. 

## **3. Proposed Approach: The 'Customer-Centric Trust Fabric'** 

- **Customer 'Privacy Cockpit':** A MyTelecom self-service hub empowering subscribers with plain-language micro-consents, tracking opt-outs, and single-click automated DSAR access or erasure. 

- **Cryptographic Tokenization & Crypto-Erasure:** An HSM-backed vault that substitutes raw PII with high-entropy tokens before ingest. Right-to-erasure triggers key destruction, rendering historical models mathematically un-linkable without retraining. 

- **Autonomous Minor Safeguard Engine:** Upstream filters cross-referencing biometric KYC data and usage heuristics to isolate minor SIMs from all behavioral profiling and commercial AI feeds. 

- **Sovereign XAI & On-Prem RAG Enclaves:** Air-gapped on-premises deployments for customer care and e-KYC, with SHAP/LIME explainability proxies supplying human-understandable audit trails for automated decisions. 

## **4. Expected Impact (Customers & Organization)** 

- **For Customers:** Total visibility and autonomy over personal data, proactive defense against predatory profiling, and ethical, transparent AI-driven customer interactions. 

- **For Organization:** 100% auditable PDPA compliance, prevention of regulatory fines, automated data lineage, and positioning New Telecom Ltd. as Bangladesh's premier trusted digital operator. 

### **5. Applicability & National Significance (Bangladesh)** 

As a Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) connecting 180M+ SIMs and driving mobile financial services (MFS), New Telecom Ltd.'s deployment of an on-premises, decentralized privacy mesh provides a benchmark for Smart Bangladesh 2041, proving that AI innovation and constitutional data rights can advance simultaneously.
